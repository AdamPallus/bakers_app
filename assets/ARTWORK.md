## Final prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small editorial illustration for a refined, minimal sourdough calculator web app, not a UI mockup. Draw one rustic oval scored sourdough loaf, a single cut slice leaning beside it, and two slender wheat stems tucked behind, as a restrained two-color hand-pulled linocut / vintage cookbook engraving. Confident slightly imperfect ink strokes, spare crosshatching, tactile printmaking quality, generous negative space, no photorealism, no 3D, no cute face. Loaf in muted burnt ochre (#b87443) with very dark indigo (#263b47) contour accents; wheat in indigo. Background completely flat warm ivory (#f6f1e7), no paper shadows, no vignette, no gradient. Landscape 3:2 composition, grouped subjects centered, filling middle 75% of width and middle 60% of height, all artwork clear of edges. Sophisticated, modest, like a small illustration in a beautiful French bakery's printed recipe booklet. No text, no letters, no typography, no border, no props beyond bread and wheat. This is a production web illustration; keep silhouettes legible at 240px wide.
